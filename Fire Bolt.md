# Fire Bolt
Cantrip — Evocation

You hurl a mote of fire at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Fire damage. A flammable object hit by this spell ignites if it isn't being worn or carried.

This spell's damage increases by 1d10 when you reach character level 5, 11, and 17 — the sheet doesn't track character-level cantrip scaling automatically, so update this note's `Damage / Effect` value by hand as the character levels up.
